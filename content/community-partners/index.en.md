# Sponsors 2025/26

## Next Digital (CompliCAUS V)

[Next Digital](https://www.nextdigital.es/) is a Spanish company specialising in software development, data engineering and technological innovation. Next Digital leads the digital transformation of businesses through solutions in Big Data, Business Intelligence, Machine Learning, cloud platforms, conversational interfaces and more. Its team focuses on supporting other organisations in their digital transformation journey, with an emphasis on innovation and design.

At CAUS, we want to express our sincerest gratitude for their decisive support in this edition. Thanks to their commitment to young talent, Next Digital has made CompliCAUS V possible by fully sponsoring the prizes for first and second place.

## School of Computer Engineering (CompliCAUS IV and V)

The School of Computer Engineering (ETSII) of the University of Seville, founded in 2001, occupies the former building of the School of Industrial Engineering, recently renovated. With an area of 24,000 m², the ETSII offers modern facilities including lecture rooms, laboratories, a library and its own computing centre.

Today, the ETSII is not only our main venue for contests, talks and work sessions, but a fundamental pillar of CAUS. We are especially grateful for its collaboration as sponsor of the CompliCAUS IV prizes, as well as for the third place and the special prize for the last problem solved in the current edition.
